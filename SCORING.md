# How Tug addresses the four Chain Jam scores

## Novelty
No crash curve, dice roll, limbo slider, or plinko board. Tug is a **stopping-time** game: a short ladder of binary VRF checks where the skill-feel is *when to bank*. That mechanic is rare on crypto casinos and is explicitly not a banned template.

## Fun (still playing after 10 hours)
- Every hold is a discrete decision with a clear 80/20 tension.
- Multiplier climb + auto-bank at 5 keeps rounds short and replayable.
- Audio creak → survive ping / snap thud / bank settle makes each outcome hit.
- Quick wager presets and instant play-again loop for session play.

## Simplicity (no manual)
One persistent rule on the stage:

> Survive a hold to climb · Bank to cash out · Snap loses the wager

Two giant actions mid-round: **Hold** (risk) vs **Bank** (secure), with odds and payouts on the buttons themselves.

## Visual & sound
- Crafted winch/rope stage (Fraunces + DM Sans, hemp rope SVG, grain, vignette) — not neon-crypto dashboard.
- Distinct states: idle / tension / fraying / snap / banked.
- Synthesized physical audio (creak, taut ping, dry snap, warm bank) — no stock jingles.
